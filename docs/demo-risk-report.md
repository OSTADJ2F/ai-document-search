# Northstar Operations Risk Report

## Executive summary

Supply chain disruption is the primary operational risk for the next fiscal
year. The company depends on two specialist component suppliers located in the
same region, increasing concentration risk.

## Financial exposure

Foreign currency volatility may reduce reported revenue and operating margin.
Management recommends quarterly hedging reviews and a documented approval limit.

## Mitigation

The recommended mitigations are to qualify a third supplier, maintain twelve
weeks of safety stock for critical components, and test the continuity plan each
quarter.
